# Deep Dives

Curated narrative records of architectural evolution — what happened, what was discovered, what changed. Deep dives are retrospective documents written after a significant period of work, capturing the arc of decisions made and the understanding that emerged.

They are not exhaustive changelogs. They identify candidate ADRs and pivots that emerged from the work, open questions that remain, and follow-up work that was identified. The goal is to make the reasoning arc visible to anyone reading the codebase later.
